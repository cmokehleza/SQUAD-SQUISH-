PRIVACY POLICY

(We protect your data like Munch protects snacks)

Last updated: January 2025

📋 QUICK SUMMARY (FOR KIDS!)

Hey there! Here's the super simple version:

✅ We DON'T collect your personal info (name, address, phone number)

✅ We DON'T sell anything about you to anyone

✅ We DON'T show you scary or inappropriate stuff

✅ We DO use a few cookies to make the website work (not the yummy kind)

If you're under 13, ask a grown-up to read the rest with you! 👨‍👩‍👧‍👦

1. WHAT WE COLLECT

Spoiler alert: We collect almost nothing. Here's the full list:

Automatically: Basic stuff like what browser you use, which pages you visit, and how long you stay. This helps us make the site better.
Cookies: Tiny files that remember things like "you already saw the loading screen" so we don't show it again. That's it!
What we NEVER collect: Your name, email, address, phone number, school, or anything that identifies you personally.

🧠 Bloop says: "We only collect data that helps us improve. Your secrets stay secret!"

2. ABOUT COOKIES 🍪

What are cookies? Small text files stored on your device. Not the chocolate chip kind (Munch already checked).
What we use them for: Remembering your quiz answers, keeping the silly meter running, and making sure the loader only plays once per visit.
No tracking cookies! We don't use cookies to follow you around the internet. That would be creepy, and we're not creepy.

⚠️ You can turn off cookies in your browser settings, but some features might get squishy and stop working.

3. HOW WE USE INFO

To make things better: We look at which pages are popular so we can create more content you love.
To fix bugs: If something breaks, anonymous data helps us figure out why.
To keep it safe: We monitor for bad behaviour (like hacking attempts) to protect everyone.

🌀 Goofy says: "I tried to use data to make more chaos. Bloop said no."

4. THIRD PARTIES

Tailwind CSS: We use Tailwind CSS (via CDN) to style our site. They may collect basic usage data. Their privacy policy.
Google Fonts: We use Google Fonts for our awesome typography. Google's privacy policy.
No advertisers! We don't run ads or use advertising networks. Your data isn't shared with marketers.

5. CHILDREN'S PRIVACY (COPPA)

COPPA compliant! We follow the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.
Parental rights: If you believe your child has provided personal information, contact us immediately and we'll delete it faster than Zippy runs.
No chat rooms! Our site doesn't have messaging, chat, or forums where kids could share personal info.

✅ Parents: We take your child's privacy seriously. Always feel free to contact us with questions!

6. DATA SECURITY

We protect your data. We use standard security measures to keep information safe. (Bloop double-checks everything.)
No system is perfect. While we try our best, no website can guarantee 100% security. Even Giggle Castle has a moat!

7. YOUR RIGHTS

You can ask us: What data we have about you (spoiler: probably nothing).
You can request: Deletion of any data we might have.
You can say no: To cookies by adjusting your browser settings.

📧 To exercise your rights, email us at privacy@squadsquish.com

8. CHANGES & CONTACT

We may update this policy. If we do, we'll post the new version here and update the date at the top.
